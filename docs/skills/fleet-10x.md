# `/fleet-10x`

- **`/fleet-10x [--repos a,b] [--min-commits N] [--days N]`** — the **ambition lens**: asks every active fleet repo *"how could this app be 10x better, even at twice the effort?"* and turns the answers into one dated report that a reader arriving cold can act on (fleet-config#1253). Everything that must not drift between runs is in `.claude/skills/fleet-10x/fleet10x.py`. `select` picks the `.fleet.toml` repos with at least 20 commits on their default branch in the last 60 days (worktrees excluded), or an explicit list; the rest are the quiet set. `brief` renders one `research-brief.md` per repo, restating read-only in full. `validate` checks each researcher's 3-7 recommendations (impact H/M/L, cost S/M/L/XL, what changes, why it's 10x, first step, `file:line`/issue evidence). `rank` sorts them across the fleet by impact over cost into a linked table. `footer` stamps wall time and the Claude quota start -> end, or `unknown`, never an estimate. The judgment is the model's: one read-only Sonnet researcher per repo, about 6 at a time, then one Opus synthesis that writes the executive summary (cross-fleet themes and the top 10 bets), the ranked table, a "how we'd do it" wave sequence, per-repo sections and one line per quiet repo. Output is machine-local under `~/.claude/hooks/state/fleet-10x/<YYYY-MM-DD>/` and never committed, because the report carries personal workflow detail. Attended, on demand; no schedule.
